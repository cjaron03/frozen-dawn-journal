Two independent controls, not one wall
1 · Show the engineering off by default. Reveals code, file stats, architecture notes, and the deep-dive entries. Nothing to do with story.
2 · Spoilers handled inline, per paragraph, blurred until clicked. A lore reveal in a friendly entry doesn't need a whole locked section.
Separating these means a spoiler-averse player and a non-technical reader aren't forced through the same door, and neither one hits a wall labelled "advanced."
